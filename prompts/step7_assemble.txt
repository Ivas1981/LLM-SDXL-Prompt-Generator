Assemble the previous steps into a single JSON object for Stable Diffusion XL.
Use only these exact keys:

{
  "subject": "<literally {prompt} followed by clothing in 4-10 words>",
  "pose": "<body position, expression, eye contact in 5-10 words>",
  "state": "<physical state in 6-12 words>",
  "environment": "<location, time, weather, props in 8-15 words>",

  "lighting": "<physical lighting in 5-10 words>",
  "camera": "<camera, lens, angle, quality in 6-12 words>"
}

Rules:
- The string "{prompt}" (with braces) MUST appear verbatim in the "subject" field. It is a placeholder for the user's appearance description.
- Do NOT replace {prompt} with words. Do NOT describe hair, skin, age, or ethnicity — {prompt} covers that.
- Use only English.
- Each field MUST be comma-separated tags only. NO full sentences. NO periods.
- Preserve compound terms as single tags. Examples: "Canon EOS R5", "Nikon D850", "24-70mm f/2.8L", "full-frame sensor", "low angle close-up". Do NOT split camera models, lens names, or technical terms into separate words.
- Do not repeat concepts already covered by other fields. Examples: avoid repeating "direct eye contact" in both pose and relationships; avoid repeating lighting details in both lighting and environment.

- In environment, separate every prop and descriptor with commas. Example: "warehouse, afternoon, overcast, forklift lights, wooden pallet, metal trash can, window".
- Keep tags concise and distinct.
- Do not write any prose outside the JSON.
- Output only the JSON object.
